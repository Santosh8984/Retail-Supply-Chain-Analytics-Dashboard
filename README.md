An interactive Power BI dashboard for analyzing retail supply chain performance across Road, Sea, Rail, and Air transportation using KPIs, DAX measures, and business intelligence techniques.

# 🚚 Retail Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive **Retail Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard** built in **Power BI** to monitor logistics performance, transportation efficiency, supplier performance, shipping costs, revenue, profit, and delivery operations.

The dashboard helps business stakeholders make data-driven decisions by providing interactive insights into supply chain performance across multiple transportation modes.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Retail companies often manage thousands of shipments across multiple transportation modes, making it difficult to monitor operational performance and identify areas for improvement.

This dashboard was developed to answer key business questions such as:

- Which transportation mode generates the highest revenue?
- Which mode has the highest shipping cost?
- Which suppliers perform best?
- Which product categories drive the most revenue?
- How does monthly revenue trend over time?

# 💼 Business Objectives

- Monitor Supply Chain Performance
- Improve On-Time Delivery
- Reduce Shipping Costs
- Increase Profitability
- Evaluate Supplier Performance
- Analyze Transportation Efficiency
